### PDR-004: Hybrid Modular Framework Architecture for Osterwalder 44-Experiment Selector
- **Date**: 2026-10-26
- **Status**: **Proposed**
- **Deciders / Stakeholders**: Lead PM (Paula), AI Harness Architect
- **Context & Problem Statement**:
  Product Managers validating new business ideas or Zone 1 Riskiest Assumptions (RATs) struggled with two failure modes: (1) manually cross-referencing the 44 experiments in Bland & Osterwalder's *Testing Business Ideas* by cost, setup time, run time, and evidence strength, and (2) running opinion-based interviews ("What people say") to test Viability risks that require behavioral commitment ("What people do/pay"). However, dumping verbose definitions of all 44 experiments into prompts or creating an isolated skill risked severe context bloat (`> 4,000 tokens`) and skill sprawl.
- **Decision Taken**:
  Implement the Experiment Selector as a compact, modular discovery framework (`frameworks/discovery/experiment-selector.md`, `< 4,000 tokens`) and integrate it directly into `/validate-idea`, `/recommend-framework`, `/apply-framework`, and `templates/validation/idea-validation.md`.
- **Alternatives Considered**:
  - *Standalone `/select-experiment` skill*: Rejected to avoid duplicating `/validate-idea` and `/recommend-framework` routing logic.
  - *Full-text 44-experiment textbook file*: Rejected due to `.context/harness-config.yaml` token compaction constraints (`max_tokens_per_file: 4000`) and cognitive overload; resolved via a compact 44-row lookup matrix with a strict Top-3 output rule.
- **Consequences & Trade-offs**:
  - *Positive*: Zero context bloat; deterministic B2B vs. B2C and Say vs. Do experiment matching; automatic Discovery-to-Validation sequencing chains with paired Sidekick execution skills.
  - *Negative / Trade-offs*: Requires keeping the 44-experiment markdown table concise rather than storing multi-page tutorials per experiment.
